Clear payments. Fair payouts.

The payment design follows your proposed ₹5 platform fee and 5% worker commission. This is a display-only settlement preview; the payment gateway and UPI QR are not connected yet.

₹

Customer pays

Example for a ₹200 service: ₹200 agreed helper fee + ₹5 platform fee = ₹205 total.

↗

Worker receives

5% commission on ₹200 is ₹10, so the helper payout would be ₹190 before any separately agreed adjustments.

✓

Platform earns

Example platform income is ₹10 commission + ₹5 platform fee = ₹15 per completed ₹200 booking.

Trust is the service

Especially when a task involves a parent, grandparent or a visit outside the home, safety must come first.

🪪

Identity checks

Before launch, implement ID verification, background screening where lawful, and clear helper profiles.

📲

Family updates

Plan for arrival/completion check-ins, in-app support, emergency contacts and a way to report concerns.

🩺

Clear service limits

Helpers can accompany and assist, but must not provide medical treatment or handle emergencies beyond their training.
